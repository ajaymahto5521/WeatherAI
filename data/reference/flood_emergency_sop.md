# Standard Operating Procedure: Flood & Severe Inundation Emergency Response

**Document ID:** SOP-ENV-002  
**Version:** 1.8  
**Regulatory Standard:** FEMA P-259 Engineering Principles / NDMA Flood Disaster Mitigation Guidelines  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Applies To:** Logistics Yards, Ground-Level Storage Facilities, Construction Basements, Sub-grade Infrastructure  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes definitive operational trigger points and mitigation actions when surface runoff, localized waterlogging, flash flooding, or river inundation threatens site safety, personnel welfare, and electrical equipment.

---

## Section 2: Water Level Triggers & Staged Emergency Response

| Alert Phase | Rainfall / Water Threshold | Threat Classification | Automated Response Actions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Phase 1: Flood Watch** | Standing surface water 5 cm – 10 cm; rainfall forecast > 50 mm / 6 hrs | Low / Precautionary | Deploy sump pumps; inspect drainage channels and debris traps. |
| **Phase 2: Flood Warning** | Standing water > 15 cm; sustained heavy rainfall rate > 25 mm / hr | Moderate / Operational Risk | Isolate ground-level electrical supplies; erect sandbag perimeter barriers. |
| **Phase 3: Critical Evacuation** | Water depth > 30 cm; rapid rising trend or flash flood siren | High / Life Threat | Immediate site evacuation to higher ground; complete power isolation. |

---

## Section 3: Detailed Safety Action Protocols

### 3.1 Drainage & Barrier Deployment (Phase 1 & 2)
- Maintenance teams must clear all perimeter runoff ditches, sediment basins, and grated drainage channels.
- Deploy rapid-inflatable flood barriers and stacked geotextile sandbags (minimum 3 layers high) around:
  - Main electrical switchgear rooms.
  - Server and telecommunications closets.
  - Hazardous chemical containment stores.
- Standby diesel-powered submersible pumps (minimum 500 GPM capacity) must be fueled, tested, and positioned at drainage collection pits.

### 3.2 Electrical Safety & Hazardous Equipment Isolation (Phase 2 & 3)
- When standing water reaches **15 cm** in exterior work zones:
  - Site Chief Electrician must disconnect non-critical low-voltage power distribution feeds.
  - Lock-out and tag-out (LOTO) all sub-grade generators, transformers, and temporary distribution panels.
- **Strict Rule:** Never enter standing water deeper than 10 cm if submerged cables or active junction boxes are present in the zone.

### 3.3 Heavy Vehicle & Machinery Relocation
- Forklifts, mobile cranes, excavators, and transport fleets must be evacuated to Designated High Ground Zones (Staging Area Alpha or elevated ramps).
- Hydraulic booms and buckets must be grounded; parking brakes engaged; fuel supply valves shut off.

### 3.4 Site Evacuation Protocol (Phase 3 Trigger: > 30 cm Water Rise)
- In the event of rapid water ingress (> 30 cm depth or flash flood sirens):
  1. Sound three long continuous blasts of the site horn.
  2. All personnel must immediately drop non-critical tools and proceed along designated Elevated Evacuation Walkways.
  3. Assembly at Muster Point C (Upper Terrace / Admin Building Level 2).
  4. Perform immediate head count using the electronic RFID roll-call sheet.
