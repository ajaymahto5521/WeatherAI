# Standard Operating Procedure: Heatwave & Extreme Temperature Safety

**Document ID:** SOP-ENV-001  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Regulatory Standard:** OSHA Technical Manual (OTM) Section III: Chapter 4 (Heat Stress) & National Weather Service (NWS) Heat Advisory Guidelines  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Applies To:** All Outdoor Industrial Sites, Construction Operations, Logistics Hubs, and Field Personnel  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines mandatory operational thresholds and risk mitigation protocols for extreme heat conditions. Outdoor personnel are vulnerable to heat exhaustion, heat syncope, and fatal heat stroke when environmental temperatures and relative humidity surpass safe physiological thresholds.

---

## Section 2: Heat Risk Thresholds & Trigger Levels

| Risk Level | Ambient Temp (°C) | Heat Index (°C) | Status Code | Operational Posture |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Normal** | < 32°C | < 32°C | **GREEN** | Standard operations; standard hydration provisions. |
| **Caution** | 32°C – 35°C | 32°C – 37°C | **YELLOW** | Increased hydration monitoring; mandatory shade availability. |
| **Warning (High Risk)** | 36°C – 39°C | 38°C – 42°C | **ORANGE** | Mandatory scheduled rest cycles; buddy monitoring system. |
| **Severe (Extreme Danger)** | >= 40°C | >= 43°C | **RED** | Suspension of non-essential outdoor work; full emergency protocol. |

---

## Section 3: Operational Action Protocols

### 3.1 Hydration & Water Provisions (All Levels Yellow and Above)
- Employers must provide a minimum of **1 liter (approx. 1 quart) of potable cold water per worker per hour**.
- Electrolyte-replenishment drinks must be distributed when continuous outdoor exposure exceeds 2 hours.
- Drinking stations must be located within 60 meters (200 feet) walking distance of all active work areas.

### 3.2 Scheduled Rest Cycles & Shade Requirements
- **Level ORANGE (36°C – 39°C):**
  - Minimum of **15 minutes mandatory shaded rest** every 60 minutes of continuous physical labor.
  - Canopies or air-conditioned rest shelters must maintain an ambient temperature below 27°C.
- **Level RED (>= 40°C):**
  - Mandatory **30 minutes rest for every 30 minutes worked** for any essential outdoor tasks.
  - Complete suspension of heavy strenuous labor (e.g., manual trenching, roof installation, asphalt paving) between **12:00 PM and 4:00 PM**.

### 3.3 Worker Acclimatization Rules
- New or returning workers (absent for > 14 days) must follow a 5-day progressive acclimatization schedule:
  - Day 1: Max 20% normal outdoor shift duration.
  - Day 2: Max 40% duration.
  - Day 3: Max 60% duration.
  - Day 4: Max 80% duration.
  - Day 5: 100% full duration.

---

## Section 4: Emergency Response & Medical Triggers
- If a worker exhibits symptoms of **Heat Stroke** (confusion, slurred speech, core body temp > 40°C, cessation of sweating, fainting):
  1. Immediately contact Site Emergency Services and dial 112 / 911.
  2. Move victim to shaded, cool area and remove outer PPE/protective gear.
  3. Apply active cooling: cold water dousing, ice packs on neck, armpits, and groin.
  4. Do not force fluids if the worker is semi-conscious or vomiting.
