# Standard Operating Procedure: Severe Wind, Gale, and Thunderstorm Safety

**Document ID:** SOP-ENV-003  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Regulatory Standard:** OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1431 (Cranes & Derricks in Construction) & UK HSE Construction Wind Safety Protocols  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-01  
**Applies To:** Tower Crane Operations, Mobile Lifting Equipment, High-Altitude Scaffolding, and Structural Erection Crews  

---

## Section 1: Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure establishes mandatory wind speed cutoff limits, tie-down requirements, and altitude evacuation rules to prevent catastrophic structural collapse, crane tip-overs, and falling object hazards caused by high atmospheric wind gusts and storm fronts.

---

## Section 2: Wind Speed Thresholds & Action Matrix

| Beaufort / Wind Scale | Wind Speed (km/h) | Wind Speed (m/s) | Threat Level | Mandatory Operational Restrictions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Light to Moderate** | 0 – 30 km/h | 0 – 8.3 m/s | Normal | Full operations permitted with standard rigging inspections. |
| **Fresh Breeze** | 31 – 44 km/h | 8.6 – 12.2 m/s | Caution | Halt handling of large sail-area materials (sheeting, glass panels). |
| **Strong Breeze (Cut-Off)** | **45 – 54 km/h** | **12.5 – 15.0 m/s** | **HIGH RISK** | **Mandatory shutdown of all crane and lifting operations.** |
| **Near Gale / Gale** | 55 – 74 km/h | 15.3 – 20.6 m/s | Severe | Halt all work at height (> 2 meters); lower scaffolding tarpaulins. |
| **Storm Force** | >= 75 km/h | >= 20.8 m/s | Critical | Total site shutdown; evacuate all temporary structures and trailers. |

## Section 3: Specialized Crane & Lifting Protocols

### 3.1 Tower Crane Weather Vaning & Cut-off Limit (45 km/h Limit)
- Sustained wind speed exceeding **45 km/h (approx. 12.5 m/s or 28 mph)** or gusts exceeding **50 km/h** mandates the **immediate cessation of all hoisting and slewing**.
- **Shutdown Procedure:**
  1. Land any suspended load immediately onto the ground or safe deck. Never leave a load suspended in high wind.
  2. Trolley the hook in close to the tower mast (within minimum radius).
  3. Raise the hook block to the upper limit switch.
  4. Disengage the slewing brake to place the crane in **free-slew (weather-vaning) mode**, allowing the jib to rotate freely like a wind vane to minimize aerodynamic resistance.
  5. Operator must descend the tower ladder using fall-arrest inertia reels.

### 3.2 Mobile Cranes & Aerial Work Platforms (MEWPs / Cherry Pickers)
- Mobile telescopic cranes must lower booms to rest saddles or minimum elevation when wind exceeds **45 km/h**.
- Scissor lifts and boom lifts must not be operated at elevated platform heights (> 3 meters) when ground wind velocity exceeds **38 km/h**.

---

## Section 4: Work at Height & Scaffolding Restrictions
- **Wind Speed > 40 km/h:**
  - Prohibit all roofing installation, cladding assembly, and curtain walling activities.
- **Wind Speed > 55 km/h:**
  - Complete evacuation of all scaffolding towers, mast climbers, and exterior suspended cradles.
  - Remove or tie back wind-catching scaffolding netting, containment sheeting, and advertising banners to prevent scaffold overloading.
- Secure all loose plywood, corrugated sheets, metal ducts, and hand tools into locked stillages or heavy lashed storage cages.

---

## Section 5: Lightning & Thunderstorm Alert Protocol
- Follow the **30-30 Rule** for convective storm activity:
  - If the flash-to-bang time (time between seeing lightning and hearing thunder) is **less than 30 seconds** (indicating lightning within 10 km / 6 miles), immediately suspend all outdoor and high-elevation work.
  - Seek shelter inside fully enclosed substantial buildings or enclosed metal vehicles.
  - Remain sheltered until **30 minutes** after the last audible thunderclap.
